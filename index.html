Set up in three steps

Appearance → Starter Sites walks you through it. The whole thing takes a few seconds, and every choice can be changed afterwards in the Site Editor.

1

Choose a starter

Thirteen complete designs, each showing its palette and what it builds.

2

Set your name and look

Site title, tagline, logo, and any of the twelve palettes with any of the five typefaces.

3

Install what it needs

If a starter benefits from a plugin you do not have, the wizard links to WordPress's own installer. It never installs anything itself.

Step two: twelve palettes and five typefaces, mixable in any combination. The starter picks a pairing; you can take any other.

163 sections, in the inserter

Patterns are the pieces the starters are assembled from. Open any page, click the inserter and drop one in — it arrives as ordinary core blocks you edit like anything else. There is no demo importer and no sample content to delete afterwards. The niche libraries are written for their niche: the church sections talk about services and giving, not free trials.

Twelve palettes, five typefaces

Colour and typography are separate style variations, so you can mix any palette with any typeface — all sixty combinations are valid and every one is contrast-checked. The five typefaces are Poppins & Nunito, Inter, Fraunces & Inter, Space Grotesk & Inter and Manrope, all self-hosted. Every colour in every pattern is a palette slug rather than a fixed hex, which is why switching palette restyles the whole site at once.

What else is in it

No framework

No page builder, no jQuery, no icon fonts, no build step. Every section is core blocks configured through theme.json.

WooCommerce templates

Shop, product, cart, checkout, order confirmation and product search, in the theme’s own rhythm rather than the plugin’s defaults.

Real contact forms

The theme detects whichever form plugin you already use — ten are recognised — and styles its fields to your palette.

Dark mode for visitors

An optional toggle that lifts your palette rather than replacing it, so a dark site still looks like your site.

Self-hosted fonts

All six families — Poppins, Nunito, Inter, Fraunces, Space Grotesk and Manrope — ship inside the theme. No request to Google Fonts, and no GDPR question to answer.

Updates like any theme

Updates arrive through Dashboard → Updates, the same as a theme from the WordPress.org directory.
